About

Move Media is a Lebanese advertising company.

We place double-sided digital LED screens on the roofs of taxis. Brands book space on the screens, and their advertisements travel through the city all day, seen by people on the street, in traffic and on the pavement.

The rooftop screen, at night.

How it works

Four steps from your file to the street.

Step 1 of 4

Your advertisement comes to us.

You send us the advertisement you would like to run on the screens.

The face of a digital LED screen, every pixel lit.

Step 2 of 4

It joins the 10-second loop.

Advertisements run as 10-second spots in a repeating loop, so yours returns to the screen regularly throughout the day.

Each spot runs for 10 seconds, then the loop moves on.

Step 3 of 4

It plays across the fleet.

The same loop runs on taxi roofs across the city, on screens that face both sides of the road.

A taxi carrying the loop through the city at night.

Step 4 of 4

Your brand travels the city.

The taxis carry your advertisement through the streets all day, past people walking, waiting and driving.

A taxi with its screen in a Beirut street.

The screen, in detail.

Why taxi tops

Four reasons the roof of a taxi works.

It goes where people are.

The advertisement moves with the taxi, through the streets where people walk, shop and wait in traffic.

Bright by day and by night.

The screens stay clear in full sun and after dark, so the advertisement is seen at every hour.

Seen from both sides of the road.

Each screen has two faces, seen at street level from either side. Each side can show the same advertisement or a different one.

Nothing to print.

Advertisements change quickly on the screens, with nothing to print and nothing to install.
