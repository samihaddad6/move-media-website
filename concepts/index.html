Move Media, version 4.
Concept round.

A. The slider

A camera on a slider moves through the depth of each photograph. Chapters are joined by a focus pull.

B. The crane

A camera that rises and travels sideways, like a crane or a tracking car. Chapters are joined by slow dissolves through black.

C. The edit

A still camera with a living lens: a rack focus, a cut on movement and a match cut from the screen to the city.
